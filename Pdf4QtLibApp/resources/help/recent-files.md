The page [Recent](/recent) lists the documents, folders and workspaces you opened recently, with the page where you stopped reading.

## Find a document

- Type into **Search by name or path…** to filter the list.
- The chips **All**, **Files**, **Folders**, **Workspaces** and **Pinned** show one kind of items; each chip shows their number.
- **Sort** orders the list by **Last opened**, by **Name** or by **Folder**.
- Sorted by **Last opened**, the list is grouped by the time: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week** and **Earlier**.

The search, the chip and the sort are kept in the address of the page, so you come back to them by **Back** and **Forward**.

## Pin a file

Click the star of a file to pin it, click it again to unpin it. Pinned files are in the section **Pinned** at the top of Recent (**Show all pinned** lists all of them) and in **Pinned Files** on the page Open, so they stay at hand also after you open many other documents. The star of a folder pins it to the **Pinned Locations** of the page Open.

## Continue where you left off

The card **Continue where you left off** shows the document you read last with its page. **Continue reading** opens it at that page. Each file in the list shows its reading progress, for example *Page 12 of 24* or *Finished*.

## More actions

The button **⋮** of a row (or a right click) opens the menu of the item:

| Item | Menu |
| --- | --- |
| File | Open, Pin (or Unpin), Show in Folder, Copy Path, Remove from List |
| Folder | Browse files here…, Show in Folder, Copy Path, Pin to Pinned Locations (or Unpin from Pinned Locations), Remove from List |
| Workspace | Open, Rename…, Delete… |

**Remove from List** removes only the entry of Recent; the file or the folder stays on the disk.

A file, which was moved or deleted, is marked **No longer exists**. Files and folders on another computer (a network share) are never checked or read by the list: they are not marked as missing and folders show no count of documents.

## Clear the history

**Clear history…** asks first (*Clear the Recent history?*), then Recent forgets, which documents, folders and workspaces you opened and where you stopped reading; **Continue where you left off** and **Restore last session** are cleared as well. The question lists what is removed, also cleared and kept. Your files are not touched, saved workspaces and pinned items are kept, unless you check **Also remove the pinned items**. **Clear Recent History** confirms it.

The number of files kept in the history is the setting **Recent files** in [Settings › General](/settings/general#recent-files) (10 by default, at most 100). When you lower it, the oldest files, which are not pinned, are removed at once; pinned files are always kept.
