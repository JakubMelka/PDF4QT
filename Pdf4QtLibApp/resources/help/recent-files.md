The page [Recent](/recent) lists the documents, folders and workspaces you opened recently, with the page where you stopped reading.

## Find a document

- Type into **Search by name or path…** to filter the list.
- The chips **All**, **Files**, **Folders**, **Workspaces** and **Pinned** show one kind of items; each chip shows their number.
- **Sort** orders the list by the time it was opened last, by the name or by the folder.
- The list is grouped by the time: Today, Yesterday, This week and older.

## Pin a file

Click the star of a file to pin it, click it again to unpin it. Pinned files are on the top of Recent and in **Pinned Files** on the page Open, so they stay at hand also after you open many other documents.

## Continue where you left off

The card **Continue where you left off** shows the document you read last with its page. **Continue reading** opens it at that page. Each file in the list shows its reading progress, for example *Page 12 of 24* or *Finished*.

## More actions

The button **⋮** of a row (or a right click) opens the menu of the item:

| Item | Menu |
| --- | --- |
| File | Pin, Show in Folder, Copy Path, Remove from List |
| Folder | Browse files here…, Show in Folder, Copy Path, Pin to Pinned Locations, Remove from List |
| Workspace | Rename…, Delete… |

A file, which was moved or deleted, is marked **No longer exists**.

## Clear the history

**Clear history…** asks first, then Recent forgets, which documents, folders and workspaces you opened and where you stopped reading; **Continue where you left off** and **Restore last session** are cleared as well. Your files are not touched, saved workspaces and pinned items are kept, unless you choose **Also remove the pinned items**.

The number of files kept in the history is the setting **Recent files** in [Settings › General](/settings/general#recent-files).
