A document opens in its own tab with a toolbar at the top, a sidebar at the left, the pages in the middle and a status bar at the bottom.

## The toolbar

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar, Save, Undo, Redo | The buttons at the left: show or hide the sidebar (F4), save the document (Ctrl+S), undo (Ctrl+Z) and redo (Ctrl+Y) a change |
| File | Save, Save as, Print and E-mail; **Properties…**, **Automatic refresh**, **Lock document**, **Settings…** and **Close document**, see [Save, print and send](/help/save-and-print) |
| View, Go to | Panels with the display of the pages and with the navigation in the document |
| Annotate, Insert | Annotations, notes, shapes, stamps, links and outline items, see [Annotate a document](/help/annotations) |
| Tools | The tools you use with the document |
| Page | The current page and the arrows to the previous and the next page. Type a page number or a page label (for example *iii*) and press Enter |
| Zoom | The zoom of the pages with the buttons **−** and **+**; type a zoom in percent and press Enter |
| Select | The tool of the pages: **Hand** (Ctrl+H, on macOS Control+H), **Select text**, **Select table**, **Screenshot** or **Extract image** |
| Search in document… | Searching the text of the document (Ctrl+F), see [Search in a document](/help/search#search-in-a-document) |

**Hand** is the mode of a new tab: a drag of the left button moves the pages, links and form fields work by a click and annotations are selected and moved. Choose **Select text** to select the text by a drag (**Select all**, Ctrl+A, chooses it too); Esc then clears the selection. **Select table**, **Screenshot** and **Extract image** work once over the chosen mode – Esc returns to it. The middle button scrolls the pages automatically in every mode, any click or Esc ends it.

While a panel of the toolbar is open, moving the pointer over another menu button opens its panel at once, as in a menu bar; **Left** and **Right** switch to the neighboring panel. A click on the button of the open panel, or **Esc**, closes it.

In a narrow window the menu buttons show only their icons, and the zoom, **Annotate** and **Insert** and the page arrows are hidden one after another; their commands stay in the panels and on the keyboard.

## The View panel

**View** sets how the pages are shown:

- **Page layout** – **Single page**, **Continuous**, **Two pages** or **Two columns** (Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+4). **First page on the right side** (Ctrl+5) shows a book with its cover alone.
- **Page colors** – **Normal colors**, **Inverted colors**, **Grayscale**, **High contrast**, **Bitonal** (black and white) or **Custom colors**. The colors apply to every document; see [Color modes](/help/color-modes).
- **Zoom and rotation** – **Fit page** (P), **Fit width** (W) or **Fit height** (H), and **Rotate left** or **Rotate right** of the view (the document is not changed). The keys P, W and H work when the pages have the focus.
- **Magnifier** – a magnifying glass under the pointer; its size and zoom are in [Settings › Viewing](/settings/viewing#magnifier-size).
- **Full screen** (F11) – the document covers the screen without the toolbar and the sidebar. Press Esc or F11 to leave it.
- **Show annotations** – shows or hides the comments, highlights and form fields in every document. It is the same setting as **Display annotations** in [Settings › Viewing](/settings/viewing#display-annotations).
- **Rendering settings** at the bottom of the panel opens [Settings › Viewing](/settings/viewing).

## Go to

**Go to** moves to the **First**, **Previous**, **Next** or **Last** page, by one line, and to your bookmarks. Type a page number or a page label into **Go to page** and press **Go** (Ctrl+G opens it). The keys of the pages work when the pages have the focus: Page Up, Page Down, Home, End and the arrows.

## The sidebar

The icons at the top of the sidebar switch its panels: the thumbnails of the pages, the outline, layers, attachments, your bookmarks, the notes, the search results, **Speech** ([read aloud](/help/read-aloud)) and the signatures of the document, see [Bookmarks and the sidebar](/help/bookmarks). Drag the edge of the sidebar to change its width. Whether a new document opens with the sidebar, and the size of its icons, are set in [Settings › General](/settings/general#show-sidebar-when-opening-documents).

## The status bar

The status bar shows the state of the document and hints of the active tool. When the document has [signatures](/help/signatures) or when some content could not be rendered ([rendering issues](/help/document-properties#rendering-issues)), the status bar says so and a click shows the details. A document with page labels shows the page number and its label, for example *Page 3 (iii) of 10*; a click on it opens **Go to**.

## Locked documents

A document you only read can be locked by **Lock document** of the **File** panel: the tab has a lock and nothing in it can be changed. Click the lock in the tab: **Unlock** allows changes again, **Edit a Copy** opens a copy, which you can change and save under a new name. The lock is remembered for the file.

## Your reading position

PDF4QT remembers the last page of every document and the view of the document. The next time you open the document, it continues on that page; see [Recent files and pins](/help/recent-files).

> [!TIP] Full screen
> Press F11 to read without the toolbar and the sidebar, Esc to return. F4 shows or hides the sidebar.
