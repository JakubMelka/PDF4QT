A collection groups the tools you use for one task, for example *Prepare for sharing* with Compress, Remove Hidden Data and Protect with Password. Collections are shown on the [Tools](/tools) page in the side column **Collections**; a click on a collection shows its tools in their order.

PDF4QT has seven built-in collections: *Prepare for sharing*, *Scan to searchable PDF*, *Fix a scan*, *Sign a contract*, *Archive documents*, *Prepare for print* and *Extract content*. You can change them and create your own collections.

## Manage collections

**Manage** at the section Collections of the Tools page opens [Manage collections](/tools/collections). The list at the left has the groups **Built-in** and **Custom**; each row says how many tools the collection has and, for a changed collection, when it was edited. Click a collection to edit it.

## Create a collection

1. Click **New collection** below the list (or **New collection…** on the Tools page).
2. Enter the **Name**. It is shown on the Tools page, so keep it short. Two collections cannot have the same name.
3. Choose an **Icon**.
4. Add tools in **Add a tool**: search the catalog by a name, an action or a keyword, then click a tool or its **+**. Enter adds the first result. Without a search text, **Suggested for this collection** offers tools, which fit the tools of the collection.
5. Click **Save collection**.

**Save collection** is available, when the collection has a name and at least one tool. You can have at most 200 custom collections.

## Change the order of the tools

The tools of a collection are shown in the order of the list **Tools in this collection**.

- Drag a tool by its handle **⋮** to another place.
- With the keyboard, focus a tool and press **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down**; **Alt+Home** and **Alt+End** move it to the top or the bottom.
- The **×** of a tool, or the **Delete** key, removes it from the collection. The tool stays in the catalog.

## Save, cancel and leave

Your changes are kept only in the editor until you click **Save collection**. **Cancel** returns to the saved collection.

When you open another collection or another page before you save, nothing is lost: **Back** (Alt+Left) returns to the editor with your changes. They are saved only by **Save collection**.

When the collection is changed in another PDF4QT window while you edit it, a bar says so: **Reload** shows the saved collection, **Keep my changes** keeps yours. When it is deleted in another window, **Save collection** creates it again.

## Built-in collections

You can rename a built-in collection, change its icon and its tools. **Reset to default** fills the editor with the original name, icon and tools; **Save collection** then restores the built-in collection. A built-in collection cannot be deleted.

## Delete a custom collection

**Delete collection** asks first. Deleting a collection never deletes the tools or your documents.
