A workspace is a named set of documents, which you open together, for example all documents of one project.

## Restore the last session

The first row of **Workspaces** on the pages Open and Recent is **Restore last session**. It shows how many documents were open, when PDF4QT was closed last time. **Restore** opens all of them again, each in its own tab.

## Save a workspace

1. Open the documents, which belong together.
2. On the page Open, click **Save open documents as workspace…**.
3. Enter a name of the workspace and choose the documents. Each workspace must have its own name.

A workspace keeps the files and their pages, not unsaved changes. A document, which has not been saved to a file yet, must be saved first.

## Open, rename and delete a workspace

- Click a workspace to open all its documents.
- The button **⋮** of a workspace has **Rename…** and **Delete…**. Deleting a workspace never deletes its documents.
- **Manage** at the section Workspaces shows all workspaces on the page Recent.

## Pinned locations

**Pinned Locations** on the page Open are folders you use often, for example a folder of a project or a cloud drive. **Add…** chooses a folder in the dialog of your system.
