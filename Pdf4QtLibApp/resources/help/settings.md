[Settings](/settings/general) adjusts PDF4QT to the way you work. Open it from the sidebar of the Home tab. The list at the left has ten sections; click a section to show its settings.

Changes are saved automatically – there is no Save or OK button. A change applies at once to all open windows and documents of PDF4QT. In the [portable mode](/help/portable-mode) the changes apply too, but they are kept only until PDF4QT is closed.

| Storage | Where your settings are saved, their backup and the files of other versions | [Where PDF4QT keeps your data](/help/data-storage) |

Most people never need more than General, Viewing and Color modes. The sections Color management and Advanced are for special cases; their defaults suit almost everyone.

## Find a setting

Type into **Search settings…** above the list of sections. The settings, which match the words, stay visible, the others are hidden, and each section shows the number of its matches. The search looks at the names and the descriptions of the settings, and also at words, which are not in the name – for example *dark mode* finds **Color scheme**. **Clear search** (or **Esc** in the field) shows everything again.

The global search (**Ctrl+K**) finds settings as well. Its result opens the section and scrolls to the setting, which is highlighted for a moment. Links of this manual to a setting work the same way.

## Restore the defaults

**Restore defaults** at the top of a section returns the settings of that section to their default values. A window lists every changed setting with its current and its default value (for example *Recent files: 20 → 10*); **Restore Defaults** confirms it, **Cancel** keeps everything. The button is disabled, when nothing of the section has been changed.

Only the shown section is restored. Your history, your workspaces and the other sections stay as they are. Restore defaults of Security keeps your trusted certificates, and Restore defaults of Shortcuts gives every command its default shortcut again. Storage has no defaults to restore.

> [!TIP] Try a setting safely
> Each change can be taken back by choosing the previous value again, or by Restore defaults of its section. Nothing in Settings changes your documents.
