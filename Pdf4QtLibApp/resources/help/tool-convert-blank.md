Blank PDF creates a new document with empty pages – a start for notes, drawings or pages you add later. Choose the page size, the orientation and the number of pages; Create PDF opens the document right away, without a result dialog.

### Step by step

1. Press Open Blank PDF. The tool opens in its own tab “New document”.
2. Choose the size in the toolbar – a standard paper size, 16:9 for presentations, a custom width and height, or the size of a page of an open document.
3. Choose portrait or landscape and the number of pages. The preview shows the page with its dimensions.
4. Press Create PDF. The new document opens in the same tab as unsaved; save it when you are ready.

### What happens to the file

- Every page of the new document has the same size; the pages are empty.
- 16:9 is a landscape page of 338.7 × 190.5 mm; Portrait turns it to 190.5 × 338.7 mm.
- Nothing is written to disk until you save the document; the open documents are not changed.

> [!WARNING] When a value is out of range
> Width and height take 1.1 to 5,080 mm and the number of pages 1 to 10,000. Outside these limits the field shows an error and Create PDF is not available.

> [!TIP] Empty pages in an open document
> Insert Pages adds empty pages to a document that is already open.

### Page sizes

| Size | Portrait |
| --- | --- |
| A4 | 210 × 297 mm |
| A3 | 297 × 420 mm |
| A5 | 148 × 210 mm |
| Letter | 215.9 × 279.4 mm (8.5 × 11 in) |
| Legal | 215.9 × 355.6 mm (8.5 × 14 in) |
| 16:9 | 190.5 × 338.7 mm, landscape 338.7 × 190.5 mm |

**Custom** shows the fields W and H in the toolbar and starts with the size shown before. Portrait and Landscape swap the width and the height of a custom size. **Same size as an open document** takes the size of the page shown in that document as a custom size.

In the fields, Up and Down change the value by one, Page Up and Page Down by ten. Undo and Redo go back and forth through your choices.
