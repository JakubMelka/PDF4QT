The page [Tools](/tools) shows every tool of PDF4QT in one place: the 46 tools in six categories, the tools you pinned, your collections and the tools you used recently.

## Find a tool

- Type into **Search tools by name, action or keyword…**. The search looks in the names, the descriptions and the keywords of the tools, without regard to case and accents, and it tolerates one typo. *join* finds Merge, *shrink* finds Compress.
- When a tool is found only by one of its keywords, its row says which one, for example *matches “combine”* instead of its description.
- The chips **All**, **Pinned**, **Pages**, **Convert**, **Edit**, **Protect & Sign**, **Optimize** and **Review** show the tools of one category. Each chip shows the number of its tools; during a search, the number of the matching tools. A chip without a match is disabled.
- When you search in one category and other categories have matches too, a line above the results counts them; **show all … matches** shows the matches of all categories.
- **Esc** in the search field clears the search. When nothing matches, **Search Everywhere** looks for the text also in the commands, the settings, your documents and this manual (see [Search](/help/search)).

The category and the search text are kept in the address of the page: the first change of the filter is a new step of the history, so **Back** (Alt+Left) returns to the whole catalog.

## Pin a tool

Click the star of a tool to pin it, click it again to unpin it. Pinned tools are shown as tiles in **Pinned tools** at the top of the page, in the order you pinned them, and on [Home](/home). The chip **Pinned** shows only them.

## Open a tool

Click a tool, a pinned tile or a recent tool, or press **Enter** on it. In this version the tools themselves are not available yet: their pages come in a later version.

## Context menu

A right click on a tool (or **Shift+F10** and the menu key) opens its menu:

- **Open** opens the tool.
- **Pin** or **Unpin**.
- **Add to Collection** lists your collections; the collections, which contain the tool, are checked. Click a collection to add the tool to it or to remove it. **New Collection…** opens the editor of a new collection with the tool.

## Collections

The side column **Collections** lists the built-in collections and yours. A click on a collection shows its tools on this page in the order of the collection; **Edit** opens it in [Manage collections](/tools/collections). **Manage** opens the list of the collections, **New collection…** creates one. See [Tool collections](/help/tool-collections).

## Recent tools

**Recent tools** lists the five tools you used last, with the time of the last use. **Clear** removes the list (after a question) from this page, Home, the Tools panel of documents and the tutorials *For you* of [Support](/support). Your pinned tools, your collections and the suggestions of the next tools are kept. **Clear** is shown only when the list is not empty.

## Keyboard

The page has a few stops of **Tab**: the search field, the chips, the pinned tiles, the grid of all tools, the collections and the recent tools. Inside of a group the arrow keys move between the items: **Up** and **Down** along the rows (also into the card below), **Left** and **Right** to the neighbor column, **Home** and **End** to the first and the last item.
