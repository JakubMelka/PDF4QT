PDF4QT can read the text of a document aloud with a voice of your operating system. Open the panel **Speech** in the sidebar.

## Read a document

1. Go to the page, where the reading should start.
2. Click **Play**. The text is read block after block, page after page, to the end of the document.
3. **Pause** stops the reading for a moment, **Play** continues it; **Stop** ends it.

The card *Now reading* shows the text being read; the words already read are darker. With **Follow the reading** (the button at the right) the document shows the page being read.

The command *Read aloud* in the commands of the window starts and stops the reading without the panel.

## The voice

Choose the **Language** and the **Voice**, and set the **Rate**, the **Pitch** and the **Volume** of the speech. They are the same settings as Settings › Speech: a change in the panel applies to all documents and is kept for the next start.

> [!NOTE] Voices of the system
> The voices come from the speech engine of your operating system (Windows, speech-dispatcher on Linux). When no engine is available, the panel says so and the reading cannot start.
