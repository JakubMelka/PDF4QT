Annotations are notes, marks and links, which lie over the pages of a document. They are a part of the PDF file, so other PDF applications show them too.

## Add an annotation

1. Open **Annotate** or **Insert** in the toolbar of the document.
2. Choose what you want to add: a text, a note, a shape, a stamp, a web link, a link to a page or an item of the outline.
3. Draw it on the page. The bar of the active tool shows its options, for example the color and the width of the line.

When you select a text in the document, a bar above the selection offers the marks of the text, for example a highlight.

## Change an annotation

- Click an annotation to select it. Its handles move it, change its size and rotate it.
- A right click opens its menu, for example **Properties**, **Hide on Page** and **Delete**.
- **Properties** opens a dialog with all its options; **Apply** changes the annotation as one step, which you can undo.
- The color and the width of a selected annotation change at once in its bar.
- Annotations can be aligned to each other and copied to more pages.

## Replies and the panel Notes

Notes can have replies. The panel **Notes** of the sidebar lists all notes of the document with their replies, and a click on a note shows it on its page.

> [!NOTE] Documents, which do not allow changes
> When the author of a document did not allow changes, or when the document is locked, annotations cannot be added or changed. **Edit a Copy** opens a copy, which you can annotate.

## Undo

Every change of an annotation can be undone by **Undo** and done again by **Redo**.
