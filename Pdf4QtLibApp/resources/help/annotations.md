Annotations are notes, marks and links, which lie over the pages of a document. They are a part of the PDF file, so other PDF applications show them too. Every new annotation is one step, which **Undo** removes.

## Mark text

1. Choose **Select text** in the **Select** menu of the toolbar and select a text with the mouse. A small bar appears above the selection.
2. Click **Highlight**, **Underline**, **Strikeout** or **Squiggly** in the bar. The text is marked at once.

The same marks are in the panel **Annotate** of the toolbar and they have shortcuts: **Ctrl+Shift+H** highlights, **Ctrl+Shift+L** underlines, **Ctrl+Shift+K** strikes out and **Ctrl+Shift+Q** draws a squiggly line. When no text is selected, the mark becomes a tool: drag over the text, which you want to mark, and press **Esc** when you are done.

The color row of the panel **Annotate** chooses the color of the active mark; each kind of mark remembers its own color. **Show all comments** opens the panel **Notes** of the sidebar.

> [!TIP] Turn the bar off
> The bar above a selection can be turned off in **Settings › Editing**: *Show markup bar when text is selected*.

## Highlight and comment

**Highlight and comment** (**Ctrl+Shift+M**) highlights the selected text and opens a small window for the comment. **Post** (or **Ctrl+Enter**) creates one highlight with the comment; **Esc** creates nothing.

## Insert a note, a text box, a shape or a stamp

Open **Insert** in the toolbar and choose a tile. A bar of the tool appears above the page with its options, for example the color and the width of the line. The tool stays active, so you can add more annotations; the close button of the bar or **Esc** ends it.

- **Text box** – drag a rectangle on the page. A window below it asks for the text, the font, the size, the color, the alignment and the border. **Add** (or **Ctrl+Enter**) adds the text box, **Esc** cancels it.
- **Comment, Note, Help, Key, Insert, Paragraph, New paragraph** – sticky notes with these icons. Click on the page and write the note.
- **Line, Polyline, Rectangle, Polygon, Ellipse, Freehand** – draw the shape. A polyline and a polygon are completed by **Enter**. Closed shapes can have a fill: choose **Fill** in the bar of the tool.
- **Stamps** – click a stamp, then click on the page.

## Add a link

- **Web link** – drag a rectangle over the linked area and enter the address. Only addresses starting with *http*, *https* or *mailto* can be added.
- **Link to page** – a link in two steps. First draw the link area. Then go to the target page – scroll, use the page field, the thumbnails or **Go to** – and click it. **Target** in the bar chooses what the link shows: the whole page, the page width, the page height, an area, which you draw, or a position. **Back** (or **Esc**) draws the link area again. When the link is added, the view returns to it.

## Mark search results

After a search, **Mark as annotations…** at the bottom of the search results marks the found texts as highlights, underlines, squiggly lines or strikethroughs – all results, the results of the current page or the selected result. All marks are one step, which **Undo** removes. Many results are marked in the background; the progress is shown in the tab and the marking can be stopped.

## Delete annotations

**Delete annotations** at the bottom of the panel **Insert** turns the pointer into an eraser: click an annotation, or drag a rectangle around annotations, to delete them.

## Change an annotation

Click an annotation to select it. Its handles move it and change its size, its bar changes the color and the width, and a right click opens its menu with **Properties**, **Hide on Page** and **Delete**. See [Edit annotations](/help/edit-annotations).

> [!NOTE] Documents, which do not allow changes
> When the author of a document did not allow changes, or when the document is locked, the panels **Annotate** and **Insert** are not available and the bar above a selection offers only **Copy**. **Edit a Copy** opens a copy, which you can annotate.
