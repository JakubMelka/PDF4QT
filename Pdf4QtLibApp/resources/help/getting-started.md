PDF4QT is a free and open-source application for PDF documents on Windows, Linux and macOS. It is one application with tabs: the Home tab gives you the pages of the application, and every document opens in a tab of its own.

## The Home tab

The Home tab is always the first tab of the window and it cannot be closed. Its sidebar has three groups of pages:

| Pages | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| Home, Open, Recent, Tools, AI | Your documents, the catalog of tools and the connection of AI assistants |
| What's New, About, Support, Sponsor | The changes of the release, information about the application, help and the ways to support the project |
| Settings | Everything you can adjust |

At the bottom of the sidebar, the switch **Light / Dark / Auto** chooses the color scheme. *Auto* follows the color scheme of your system. It is the same setting as **Color scheme** in [Settings › General](/settings/general#color-scheme).

## Open your first document

1. Click **Open** in the sidebar.
2. Drop PDF files on the area *Drop PDF files here*, or click **Browse Files…** and choose them.
3. Each document opens in its own tab. The Home tab stays where it is, so you can go back to it at any time.

You can also open documents from the file manager of your system or from the command line, see [Open documents](/help/open-documents).

## Move around like on the web

Every page of PDF4QT has an address, for example `/settings/general`. You can go back and forward between the pages you have visited:

- **Alt+Left** goes back, **Alt+Right** goes forward.
- The back and forward buttons of your mouse do the same.
- A link to a page, which does not exist, shows *Page Not Found* with the buttons **Back**, **Search for…** and **Go to Home**.

> [!TIP] Search everything
> Press **Ctrl+K** to search tools, commands, settings, documents and this manual from any page with a search field. See [Search everything](/help/search).
