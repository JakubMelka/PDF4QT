A PDF form has fields, which you can fill in directly on the page: text fields, check boxes, radio buttons and lists of choices.

## Fill in a form

1. Open the document with the form.
2. Click a field and type its value, or choose a value of a list. Check boxes and radio buttons change by a click.
3. **Tab** moves to the next field.
4. Save the document by **Save**, or save a filled copy by **Save as**.

## Options

The behavior of forms is a part of [Settings › Editing](/settings/editing).

> [!WARNING] Signed documents
> Filling in a form changes the document. When the document is signed, PDF4QT asks, before it saves the changes, because a change after the signature is shown in the signatures of the document.
