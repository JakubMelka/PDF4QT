## Overview

PDF4QT 1.5.3 (1.5.3.1) fixes the Windows installer. It replaces 1.5.3.0, which was removed, and contains its 8 resolved issues: AppImage update information, a Traditional Chinese translation, removal of external links, nested bookmarks, which keep their children, pictures in signatures, fonts on Linux and drag and drop of text notes.

## Fixed issues

- #341 · Embed AppImage update information
- #339 · (zh_TW)Traditional Chinese Translation File
- #333 · ijg-libjpeg obsoleted in vcpkg
- #329 · How to remove "External links"
- #269 · Nested Bookmarks loses children when moving.
- #179 · Signature: Adding .jpg or .png image crashes PDF4QT
- #146 · Incorrect fonts displayed on Linux
- #144 · Text notes - drag and drop annotations
