## Overview

PDF4QT 1.2.0 adds static XFA support (read only, simple XFA only) and electronic and digital signing of documents, and it resolves 3 issues: performance optimization, text of vertical writing systems and an .msi installer.

## Fixed issues

- #10 · Performance optimization
- #14 · Incorrect text drawing for vertical writing systems
- #15 · .msi installer

## Release notes

The changes of PDF4QT 1.2.0 as they are recorded in RELEASES.txt of the repository.

### Changes

- static XFA support (readonly, simple XFA only)
- electronically/digitally sign documents
